# Voice 02 — Wandering (experimental)

*Influences: Glenn Gould, "The Idea of North" (wandering, layered, contrapuntal
dialogue; rambling moods); Herman Melville (digression that detours and earns it);
sandwiched with Scalzi / Douglas Adams "Last Chance to See" clarity. the author's own gloss:
"wandering but concise, with some blunt irony."*

**Status: experimental and unproven.** This voice reintroduces the digressive thread that
the default voice deliberately suppresses. Try it on a throwaway project first. It is the
one to tinker with; do not let tinkering here leak into `01-direct`.

This file governs **register only**. The `base-*` mechanics it pairs with still hold —
the prose can wander, but it still builds, still cites correctly, still bans filler words.

---

## The core idea

The default voice leads with the point and keeps one idea per paragraph. This voice does
the opposite *on purpose, in controlled doses*: it lets a thought open out, follows a
tangent that illuminates, and then lands. The discipline is the landing. A digression that
doesn't return is just a mess; a digression that returns with something the reader didn't
have before is the whole appeal.

Think of it as Gould's contrapuntal radio: several lines of thought running at once,
braided, never quite resolving until they do. Then — Adams — a flat, clear, slightly
ironic sentence that catches the whole thing and sets it down.

---

## Voice and tone

**Wandering but concise.** Sentences may roam; the paragraph still has a destination. The
roaming is texture, not padding. If a tangent can be cut with no loss, it was padding.

**Blunt irony.** Dry understatement with a harder edge than `01-direct` — willing to name
the absurd thing plainly. Still affectionate, never cynical.

**Conversational and digressive, then sharp.** The signature move: a longer, layered,
exploratory passage *sandwiched* against a short, clear, easy-to-digest statement that
resolves it. The clarity is what licenses the wandering.

**Speak to the reader directly.** Still "you." The reader is a companion on the ramble,
not a passenger.

---

## Sentence and paragraph style

**Longer paragraphs are allowed here** — a single paragraph may carry a thought through
two or three turns. But it must turn; a paragraph that only accumulates is not wandering,
it's a list with the seams hidden.

**Resolve every detour.** End a wandering passage with a short sentence that makes the
detour pay. This is the Adams clamp on the Gould drift.

**Still no throat-clearing, still no filler words.** Wandering is not an excuse for
"it is important to note." The base's banned words still apply.

**Em dashes do heavy lifting** — they are the joints of a layered sentence.

---

## How it differs from 01-direct (quick contrast)

| | 01-direct | 02-wandering |
|---|---|---|
| Paragraph shape | one idea, lead with point | one *arc*, point may arrive last |
| Tangents | cut them | follow the ones that pay, then land |
| Humor | dry, incidental | dry with a blunter ironic edge |
| Risk | none (proven) | a drift that never resolves |
